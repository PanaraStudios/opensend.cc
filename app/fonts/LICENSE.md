# Bundled fonts

Latin-subset woff2 files, downloaded from Google Fonts and self-hosted so
production builds never fetch fonts from Google.

- Bricolage Grotesque (variable, wght 200-800 + opsz) - The Bricolage Grotesque Project Authors
- Geist Mono (variable) - The Geist Project Authors (Vercel)
- Instrument Serif (Italic 400) - The Instrument Serif Project Authors

All three are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org). The fonts may be used, bundled and
redistributed freely, provided this notice stays with them and they are not
sold on their own.
